Things to avoid

Don't
Distort, skew, or stretch the mark. Always lock proportions.

Don't

Do
Give the mark ½ its own height in clear space, and let it breathe.

The motifs

Each piece of the mark can stand on its own as a decorative element — empty states, section dividers, swag, OG images. Used line-only so the mark stays the only "filled" version.

Cloud

Self-hosted. The cloud you built yourself, not one you rent. Used near hosting, deployment, and sync.

Open book

The library. Used near scan, import, metadata, and any view of the collection itself.

Waveform

Playback. The "now" of the product. Animates left-to-right when audio is active. Used in the player and on covers.

Headphones

The listener. Used for personal moments — account, sessions, profile, sleep timer.
